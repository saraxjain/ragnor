Impact Pod

A behind-the-ear sensor that notices a head impact and starts the 7-day symptom follow-up in the app. This is a design model only. Nothing here has been built or tested.

Design targets (assumptions, not measurements)

38 × 24 × 9 mmPod size, small enough to sit behind the ear
Under 8 gLight enough that the adhesive holds through a practice
~10 g triggerStarting threshold for recording an impact, tuned in the pilot
1 weekBattery life between charges, asleep unless hit

How a hit becomes a follow-up

  1. ImpactThe accelerometer crosses the threshold and wakes the chip.
  2. RecordA short window of motion data around the hit is saved to memory.
  3. SyncThe pod sends the event to the athlete's phone over Bluetooth.
  4. Follow upThe app starts a daily symptom check-in for 7 days.
  5. AlertThe trainer sees the event and any red-flag symptoms.

The pod only reports that a hit happened. Impact size alone does not show whether someone has a concussion, so the pod never diagnoses and never clears an athlete. A clinician decides.
